About CCE Business Solutions

Project management and delivery leadership grounded in real-world experience.

CCE Business Solutions was founded on a straightforward premise: that the difference between a project that succeeds and one that stalls almost always comes down to leadership. Not tools. Not frameworks. Leadership.

Our Background

Decades of hands-on experience in the work that matters.

Our team brings decades of experience managing complex technical programs across infrastructure, cloud services, digital transformation, enterprise software, and security. We have led multi-million-dollar engagements for organizations across all verticals and sizes — from professional services firms and public sector agencies to energy companies and technology enterprises operating at scale.

That experience has taught us something frameworks don't capture well: every organization is different. What transfers is the discipline of clear thinking, honest assessment, and steady execution.

Eddie Monroe

Managing Partner

Our Philosophy

The gap is not in planning.
It's in execution.

There is no shortage of firms that can produce a Gantt chart and run a status meeting. Plans are only as good as the leadership behind them, and execution requires someone willing to make hard calls, surface problems early, and hold the line on accountability when it would be easier to let things slide.

That is the space CCE occupies. We assess the real situation, understand the organizational dynamics, and build a delivery approach that fits. Our clients value us not because we follow a process, but because we own the outcome.

Our DNA

The DNA of CCE

Eight strands that define how we work, how we think, and what we bring to every engagement.

01

Culture

Positive mindset. Integrity and Ethics. Approachable demeanor that lets everyone know we can get through whatever project obstacle as a team.

02

Customer Experience

Journey focused with mature professionalism. Not just get to the finish line, but the manner in which we get there is just as important.

03

Calm

Even keeled. Being frazzled gets us nowhere. Thinking situations through logically prevails. There is an answer to everything but it takes a sound mind to get the solution correct.

04

Teamwork

Solo is not the answer. Coming together as a unit brings about the best results. Communication, responsiveness and documentation can solve any obstacle.

05

6th Sense

Consultative intuition. There is a sense that one must possess that you cannot document or plan for. Your gut tells you the way to go because of experience and knowledge.

06

Run to the Fire

Confrontation is okay. No project is 100% smooth. When trouble arises, our mindset is to run to it, own it, figure it out and move to the next task.

07

Adaptability

Pivot as needed. No blueprint is expected but the project mindset to adjust is. Being flexible and open to adapting to any environment.

08

Finish

Nothing lasts forever. Projects shouldn't either. Important to get to the finish line and accomplish whatever goal set. Without finishing, the goals are irrelevant.

What We Value

Three principles that guide every engagement.

Clarity over complexity

The best project leadership simplifies. We cut through noise: identifying what matters, what's blocking progress, and what happens next.

Accountability over activity

Dashboards aren't progress. We measure our work by whether the initiative moved forward and whether leadership has confidence in the path ahead.

Candor over comfort

We tell clients what they need to hear. If a timeline is unrealistic, we say so. Directness paired with credibility is how we operate.
